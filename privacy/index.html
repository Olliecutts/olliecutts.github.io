Privacy notice

This notice explains the processing of Google and YouTube data by King Chilli Comment Starter, a private tool operated by King Chilli The Pug. The application is not offered as a public sign-up service.

1. Who operates it

King Chilli The Pug operates the application for its own authorised YouTube channel. Questions and requests about personal information, access or deletion can be sent to hello@kingchillithepug.com.

2. Information accessed

Comment Starter uses YouTube API Services with permission of the connected channel owner. Depending on the permitted operation, it accesses the authorised YouTube channel identifier, metadata for videos owned by that channel (including video identifiers, publication times and descriptions), top-level comments, their author-channel references and timestamps, existing replies and the application's own published replies.

It examines the platform-rendered comment display temporarily to identify only narrowly whitelisted, positive emoji-only reactions. Such displayed text is not treated as guaranteed original text. Written comments, questions and ambiguous material are not eligible for automated replies.

The channel owner authorises access using Google's OAuth system. The application does not collect or store a Google account password. OAuth credentials, where provisioned, are kept in private operator-controlled files and are not published on this website.

3. Why data is used

The information is used to validate the connected channel and its owned videos, decide whether an eligible comment may receive a response from a fixed collection of pre-approved messages, prevent repeat replies, enforce rate limits and provide an audit trail for uncertain or completed actions.

Comment Starter is not used to profile viewers, send marketing messages, trade in personal data, or generate AI-written replies. It does not request access to anyone else's Google account through this informational website.

4. Data kept locally

The application's private action ledger stores limited operational records such as video and comment identifiers, keyed digests of commenter-channel identifiers, timestamps, selected reply identifiers, and the state of attempted or completed responses. These durable records help stop duplicate replies and preserve evidence when a YouTube action has an uncertain outcome. It is not designed to preserve a copy of the full viewer comment body in its audit ledger.

These operational records are not placed in this GitHub Pages website. They remain in operator-controlled local storage subject to access restrictions. They are retained until removed through the relevant operator process; the current safety ledger should not be interpreted as automatically deleting every audit record after a fixed period. YouTube API data remains subject to Google's refresh, retention and deletion requirements.

5. Sharing and website hosting

Information needed to read comments or publish an authorised reply is exchanged with Google and YouTube through their official API services. We do not sell viewer information or disclose private Comment Starter audit records to advertisers. This informational website uses no site-operated advertising, analytics scripts, or marketing cookies.

The public information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary website access and security logs under its privacy statement.

6. Revocation and deletion requests

The channel owner can review and revoke connected Google permissions via Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops authorised access; it is not an instruction to retry failed actions. You can also contact hello@kingchillithepug.com to request deletion or review of information held by the application, including information relating to a YouTube comment.

Requests are assessed under applicable data-protection law and YouTube API policies. Some deletions require the operator to pause the application and manually reconcile the safety ledger; there is currently no public self-service deletion dashboard. We will not represent private audit data as deleted until the relevant process has been completed.

Google's own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube's rules and rights are set out in the YouTube Terms of Service.

7. Your rights and changes

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, restrict or delete personal data about you, or object to processing. Please use the contact address above so that a request can be assessed without disclosing account credentials or private comment history publicly.

We may update this notice if the application's YouTube access or data-handling behaviour changes. The date at the top identifies this version.

This notice covers the private Comment Starter application. It is separate from any privacy notice governing purchases, subscriptions or newsletters on the King Chilli public website.
