Terms of use

King Chilli Comment Starter is an internal application for the authorised operator of the King Chilli The Pug YouTube channel. These pages describe its intended use; they do not offer a public software subscription or account.

1. Permitted use

Only the channel owner and their expressly authorised operators may connect or operate the application. There is no public account creation, payment, or third-party entitlement to run it. Viewing this informational site does not give visitors access to the connected YouTube channel or the application.

2. YouTube integration

The application uses official YouTube API Services. By authorising and using its YouTube integration, the operator agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and applicable YouTube API Services Terms and policies. Google's handling of account information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Comment Starter is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

3. Responses and safeguards

The application is designed to consider a very small category of positive, emoji-only viewer comments on verified, recent videos belonging to the connected channel. It selects responses from a pre-approved fixed reply bank. It withholds questions, health or welfare topics, criticism, ambiguous content and ordinary written comments.

Its initial pilot is restricted to at most five attempted replies in any rolling 24 hours, with further per-video, per-commenter, quiet-hour and duplicate-prevention controls. The operator can pause it. Where a submission's result is unknown, the application is intended to stop further sends rather than blindly retry the same comment.

The presence of this public information page does not mean the automated pilot has been activated or that a reply is guaranteed.

4. Authorisation and control

The connected YouTube owner grants permission through Google OAuth and may revoke that permission via Google Account connections. The operator remains responsible for authorising any actual publication, keeping credentials private, observing provider restrictions and stopping the application if eligibility or account ownership becomes uncertain.

5. Availability and restrictions

This private tool may be disabled, limited or discontinued for security, provider-quota, compliance or operational reasons. It may not be used to access channels without permission, harvest comments, misrepresent the channel, or evade YouTube's policies or restrictions. No uptime or availability commitment is offered to visitors.

6. Privacy, contact and changes

The Comment Starter Privacy Notice explains what YouTube information is accessed, how it is used, what limited records are stored and how to request deletion. For questions about this application or its terms, contact hello@kingchillithepug.com.
